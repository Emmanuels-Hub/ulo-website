Corporate FAQs

Can we customize the content?

Yes. For Enterprise plans, you can upload your own training materials (PDFs, videos, docs) and our AI will generate quizzes, summaries, and learning paths based on your proprietary content.

Is my company data secure?

Absolutely. We use enterprise-grade encryption and adhere to strict data privacy policies. We are happy to sign NDAs and provide security documentation for your IT compliance review.

Do you offer Single Sign-On (SSO)?

Yes, we support SSO via SAML, Google Workspace, and Microsoft Azure AD to ensure seamless and secure access for your employees.
