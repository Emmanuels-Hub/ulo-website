Partner FAQs

What types of content can we integrate?

We support PDFs, ePubs, Word documents, MP4 videos, and SCORM packages. Our AI can process almost any digital educational format.

How do we track revenue?

Partners get access to a dedicated dashboard showing real-time views, quiz completions, and estimated revenue earnings based on your specific agreement.

Does ULO AI own our content?

No. You retain 100% ownership of your IP. ULO AI simply acts as a distribution and enhancement platform. You can remove your content at any time.
