Common Questions

How much time does it take?

We recommend about 3-5 hours per week. This includes planning events, sharing on social media, or attending our monthly catch-up calls. It's flexible around your studies!

Do I need to be a tech expert?

Nope! We look for passion, leadership, and communication skills. Whether you study Law, Art, or Engineering, if you love learning, you're a fit.

Is this a paid role?

This is a volunteer leadership program. However, top performers earn stipends, gift cards, and are fast-tracked for paid internships at ULO AI.
