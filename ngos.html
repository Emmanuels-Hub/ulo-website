Questions NGOs Ask

Can ULO AI work without internet?

Yes. We have low-bandwidth modes and offline-sync capabilities specifically designed for rural deployments. Students can complete assignments offline, and data syncs when connectivity is restored.

How do we measure impact?

We provide a Partner Dashboard that tracks granular metrics: time spent learning, quiz scores, improvement over time, and completion rates. These can be exported for donor reports.

Can the content be localized?

Absolutely. ULO AI supports multiple curricula (WAEC, JAMB, Cambridge, etc.) and can be adapted to include local languages or specific programmatic content provided by the NGO.
