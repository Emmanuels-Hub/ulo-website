About Ulo

Our Mission is to revolutionize the learning experience by equipping students with intelligent, affordable, and accessible AI-powered tools that support them anytime and anywhere.

Our Vision is to become the leading AI-powered education companion in Africa and beyond, ensuring that every learner — regardless of location or background — has access to personalized academic support.

ULO AI is currently headquartered in Nigeria and is building its reach across Africa, aiming to empower millions of students through digital learning innovation.
